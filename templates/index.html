🧠 Did You Know? — Financial Literacy Corner

Expand your money intelligence with these key rules of wealth accumulation.

➗
The Rule of 72
Divide 72 by your annual interest rate to find out exactly how many years it takes to double your money. E.g. at 12% returns, money doubles in 6 years.
📊
The 50/30/20 Rule
A standard budgeting rule of thumb: allocate 50% of monthly income to Needs (essential bills), 30% to Wants (leisure), and 20% to Savings & investments.
🛡️
Emergency Buffer
Before making risk-heavy investments, aim to accumulate at least 3 to 6 months of absolute basic expenses in a high-interest savings or liquid mutual fund.
🌱
Power of SIPs
Starting a Mutual Fund SIP early yields compounding benefits. Investing ₹5,000/month at 12% returns for 20 years builds an impressive wealth of over ₹50 Lakhs.
